Faceoffs

The food chain

When two storms meet they lock together and spin round each other, the way real hurricanes do (the Fujiwhara effect), draining each other dry. The one that's strong against the other hits half as hard again.

  1. Sandstorm beats Hurricane

    Dry desert dust starves a hurricane.

  2. Hurricane beats Wildfire

    Torrential rain drowns a fire.

  3. Wildfire beats Blizzard

    Fire melts the snow.

  4. Blizzard beats Tornado

    Cold, stable air kills a tornado’s updraft.

  5. Tornado beats Supercell

    A tornado tears the storm’s updraft apart.

  6. Supercell beats Sandstorm

    Rain washes the dust out of the air.

How it plays

Feed. Wreck. Brawl.

01 Feed

Your energy is your health and your size. Warm ocean makes a hurricane a category 5; land tears it apart. A tornado wants the open plains, a blizzard the frozen north. The big map shows where you thrive.

02 Wreck

Every city under you pays out in dollars, and the countryside too. Hit a city again and it gives less, until it rebuilds. Real coastlines and 826 real cities, from Tokyo to Lagos.

03 Brawl

Boost into a rival to ram it, drain it in a Fujiwhara lock, finish it with your special. A storm at zero energy is gone for four seconds, and whoever took it out grows stronger.
